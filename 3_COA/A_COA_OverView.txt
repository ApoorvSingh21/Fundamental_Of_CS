5. Understand signed and floating-point representation.
6. Understand memory hierarchy and cache organisation.
7. Solve cache mapping and AMAT problems.
8. Understand virtual memory and address translation.
9. Understand interrupts and DMA.
10. Understand instruction execution in detail.
11. Design and analyse basic datapaths.
12. Understand hardwired and microprogrammed control.
13. Solve CPU performance problems.
14. Analyse instruction pipelines.
15. Solve pipeline hazard problems.

18. Understand multicore and parallel architectures.
19. Understand modern CPU/GPU architecture.

21. Understand RISC-V architecture.
22. Solve B.Tech university-level COA questions.
23. Solve GATE/competitive-exam-style COA problems.
